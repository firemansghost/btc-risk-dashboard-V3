## v1.1.1 integrity changes

Recorded here as implementation semantics. This is not a rewrite of factor methodology.

### Snapshot semantics

GhostGauge is a daily UTC **intraday snapshot** model.

Fields:

- `as_of_utc`
- `snapshot_date`
- `price_kind = utc_intraday_snapshot`

`daily_close_date` remains a deprecated compatibility alias.

Do **not** call the current BTC value an end-of-day daily close.

### Canonical BTC history

`public/data/btc_price_history.csv` represents **completed UTC daily candles**.

Rules:

- the open current-day candle is excluded
- contiguous completed dates are validated
- stale or gappy history triggers a full Coinbase backfill
- canonical writes are repository-safe and atomic
- no synthetic date filling

Trend:

- snapshot price may be the current intraday value
- the SMA200 denominator uses completed daily closes

### Completed weekly semantics

Sunday becomes a completed weekly observation only after Monday 00:00 UTC.

BMSB / weekly RSI use completed weeks.

Market Regime uses completed weekly closes and remains **display-only**.

### Source provenance

Source observation time is distinct from fetch/cache time.

Do not stamp stale source content with fetch-now simply to make it look fresh.

### CoinGecko cache

Disk-cache TTL is based on the explicit content field `cachedAt`.

Filesystem `mtime` is **not** freshness authority.

Legacy raw disk-cache payloads without a trustworthy `cachedAt` are cache misses.

### Term freshness

Provider/source cadence is evaluated rather than generic wall-clock TTL alone.

Funding cadence remains provider-aware.

Binance 451 is degraded-safe because BitMEX/OKX fallback remains available.

### Macro freshness

Macro preserves real vintages for:

- DTWEXBGS
- DGS2
- VIXCLS

Freshness is evaluated against each source’s appropriate publication/observation cadence.

Fetch time does not replace source vintage.

### ETF freshness

ETF rows must be eligible under U.S. trading-day / publication cadence.

Pending or partial rows cannot become finalized aggregate flow observations.

A future source timestamp cannot silently pass freshness.

### Future timestamps

Materially future source observations fail with `future_source_timestamp`.

Do not clamp them to now.

### SSOT subweights

Active scored factors consume `config/dashboard-config.json` subweights. Validation fails loudly on malformed active blends.

### Adjustments

Cycle and spike adjustments are explicitly disabled and honored as disabled.

They were not repaired or reactivated.

### Bands

Band handling is half-open / non-overlapping for fractional composite values.

### Signals

Signal v2 is the current stable-key forward signal contract.

Legacy signal files remain historical/legacy and are not rewritten.

These are post-transition debt items, **not** closeout blockers. They were not fixed in this documentation pass.

`scripts/etl/factors/trendValuation.mjs` still uses an out-of-repo relative cache path for `trend_valuation`.

Do not fix during docs closeout. Moving it could alter persistence behavior and needs a separate controlled change.

### B. Bundle tracker

Current bundle tracking has known measurement/workflow debt:

- total-size calculation double-counts chunk bytes
- “gzip” is currently an estimate based on total × 0.3 rather than measured compressed output
- CRITICAL labels do not necessarily fail the workflow
- the bundle workflow has used Node 18 despite the application engine targeting Node 20.18.x

Do not fix here.

### C. 2027 market calendars

Existing future-calendar maintenance remains required before January 2027.

The new Macro/ETF calendar semantics make this especially important.

### D. Methodology documentation reconciliation

They are **not** broadly rewritten in this closeout. They carry a 2026-08-18 continuity notice and are marked for a separate controlled documentation reconciliation.
