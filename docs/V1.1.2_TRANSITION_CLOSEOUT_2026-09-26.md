## What changed

### ETF

- The live production source changed from legacy Farside HTML acquisition to SoSoValue.
- The scored universe is the exact approved 12-name set. `FBTC` and `BTC` remain distinct identities.
- The canonical ETF unit is USD.
- Finality uses the most recent completed U.S. trading session strictly before the current America/New_York market date.
- Durable SoSoValue history is maintained.
- Frozen Farside historical calibration remains calibration provenance.
- Farside is not the live automatic fallback.
- CoinGlass is not the live automatic fallback.

### Macro VIX

- The primary VIX provider is direct official Cboe daily VIX history.
- FRED `VIXCLS` remains a guarded fallback.
- The fallback is usable only when it satisfies the same unchanged freshness contract.
- Freshness was not relaxed.
- No Macro score formula or Macro weight changed.
